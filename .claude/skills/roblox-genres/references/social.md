# Social: hangout, roleplay, party games, social deduction

## Hangout / roleplay
- **Fantasy:** being someone, somewhere, with others. **Keep:** expressive tools (emotes, outfits, props, homes), spaces that suggest scenarios (a diner, a school, a hospital), soft goals. **Failure:** empty servers (needs good activity with 2–5 players), griefing (permissions, personal space), moderation gaps (text filtering required, voice per policy).
- Strongest Roblox-native genre for retention through friends; the content is *other players*, so design *prompts for interaction*, not content to consume.

## Party / minigame collections
- **Keep:** instantly readable rules (≤ 10 s to understand), short rounds, mixed skill outcomes so everyone wins sometimes, spectating while dead. **Failure:** long waits between rounds, one dominant player, minigames that feel identical.

## Social deduction
- **Fantasy:** reading people. **Keep:** information asymmetry, meaningful tasks that create alibis, discussion phases. **Failure:** discussion impossible on mobile (support quick-chat and voting UX), meta-gaming via external voice (design tells that are in-game).
- **Roblox:** roles assigned server-side and never replicated to other clients until revealed (exploiters read everything replicated).
